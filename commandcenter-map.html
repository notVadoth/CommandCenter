Sources & licences
  • Borders: Natural Earth (public domain) for countries, de facto view; geoBoundaries gbOpen (CC BY 4.0, per-country source licences apply) for provinces and districts. Runfola D. et al. (2020), "geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries", PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866.
  • Base maps: Esri (see the credit in the map corner); OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA) with © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), SRTM.
  • Economic figures and charts: World Bank, World Development Indicators (CC BY 4.0); each figure links to its indicator.
  • Leaders and cabinets: Wikidata (CC0). Legislature seats: the chamber's Wikipedia infobox (CC BY-SA 4.0), linked from each chamber.
  • Contested control, occupation, DMZs, disputes: sources and dates are given on each area when you click it; full list and licence notes in CONTROL_SOURCES.md. Outlines are approximate.
  • War fronts: districts flagged by this site's daily news check (headline titles from Bing News and outlet RSS feeds), placed with Wikidata coordinates and geoBoundaries districts. Each flag lists its articles. For Ukraine, compare with DeepStateMap.live (linked only; its data is not used).
  • Blocs: built-in list as of Nov 2026, checked daily against Wikidata and news reports; a dashed outline needs 2+ independent outlets. Each claim links to its articles.
  • Headlines: titles and links from each outlet's public RSS feed (daily list) and from Bing News search (place headlines). Stories belong to their publishers; only titles are shown, each linking to the original.
  • Markets: live charts by TradingView. Daily Brent closes: U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED. Index data is not stored or republished.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), from national weather services' models. Icons: "Weather elements" set by Dewanto.
  • Map engine: Leaflet (BSD-2).
Figures and outlines can be out of date or contested; check the linked source before relying on them.
